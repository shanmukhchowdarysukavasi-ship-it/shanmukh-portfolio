S-02 / DESIGN NOTES

Hello, I'm Shanmukh.

I've been working in structural drafting and detailing since 2019, building experience across high-rise residential structures, RCC detailing, water and wastewater infrastructure, and multidisciplinary engineering drawings.

My career began with an opportunity in drafting, and over the years I've learned by working through increasingly complex projects — from water-retaining structures and treatment plants to villas and high-rises with shear walls, transfer slabs, flat slabs and conventional framing.

MARGIN NOTE A drawing that's useful on site must first be technically correct — but correctness alone doesn't always make it construction-friendly.

When I work on a structure, I try to understand how the members connect, how the reinforcement will fit, and how the structure can actually be built. When something looks questionable — a member arrangement, available space, reinforcement — I take the time to understand why, and raise it with the engineer when it matters.

My goal is to combine this detailing mindset with BIM and digital workflows, so coordination and constructability come through clearly, before a single crew reaches site.
